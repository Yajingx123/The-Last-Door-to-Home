# Concept v0.1 — The Last Door to Home

## 1. Working Title
**English title:** The Last Door to Home  
**Chinese title:** 咫尺归途

---

## 2. Game Overview

The Last Door to Home is a **small 2D narrative puzzle game with branching endings**, set inside a deserted research base on an unknown planet.

The player controls an astronaut who wakes up alone after a failed return mission. The base appears intact, but something feels wrong. Messages from loved ones suggest that help is waiting outside, while fragments of other communications hint at a different truth.

By exploring rooms, collecting clues, and solving puzzles, the player gradually realizes that the safest path may not be the obvious one, and that trusting the wrong signal could lead directly to death.

The game focuses on **tension, uncertainty, and decision-making** under incomplete information.

---

## 3. Core Premise

The player believes that escape is close and that trusted voices are guiding the way home.

However, as evidence accumulates, contradictions begin to appear. Some messages are genuine attempts to help, while others are traps designed to push the player toward danger.

The central question becomes:

**Who should be trusted, and what does “going home” really mean?**

---

## 4. Player Role

The player takes the role of:

- an astronaut
- a survivor trapped in a confusing and hostile situation
- someone experiencing memory loss and fragmented perception
- someone **determined to return home** to their family

---

## 5. Player Goal

The immediate goal is to:

- explore the base
- find energy sources needed to activate the portal
- survive encounters with hostile creatures

The deeper goal is to:

- understand what truly happened during the failed mission
- distinguish between real and false guidance
- make a final decision about how to escape

---

## 6. Intended Player Experience

The intended experience is:

- curiosity at the beginning
- cautious exploration
- growing suspicion and unease
- tension during critical decisions
- a final outcome shaped by the player's understanding

The game should feel focused and suspenseful, offering a complete but compact experience rather than a large-scale adventure.

---

## 7. Tone and Theme

### Tone

- psychological tension
- mystery
- isolation
- uncertainty
- restrained horror

### Themes

- trust versus deception
- the desire to return home
- survival and determination
- perception versus reality
- the cost of misunderstanding the truth

---

## 8. Core Mechanic

The core mechanic is:

- explore connected rooms
- interact with objects and messages
- identify contradictions in information
- solve small puzzles to progress
- make decisions that influence the final outcome

This is not intended to be a fast action game.  
The main gameplay comes from observation, reasoning, and careful decision-making.

---

## 9. Core Gameplay Loop

A rough gameplay loop:

1. explore a room
2. inspect objects and collect clues
3. receive or discover new information
4. solve a small puzzle
5. reassess what information can be trusted
6. move closer to a possible ending

---

## 10. Planned Structure

Current first-pass plan:

- a small connected map of several rooms inside a research base
- a sequence of exploration and puzzle-solving phases
- one major confrontation near the end of the game
- one key decision point affecting the outcome
- three possible endings based on player understanding and choices

---

## 11. Planned Endings

### Ending 1 — Fatal Mistake

The player follows misleading guidance too early and leaves the base, resulting in immediate death.


### Ending 2 — Incomplete Understanding

The player defeats the threat but fails to fully understand the situation, leading to a disturbing and uncertain outcome.


### Ending 3 — True Escape

The player discovers the real nature of the threat, makes the correct decisions, and successfully returns home.

---

## 12. Scope Intention

This project is intended to be:

- small in scale
- achievable within the coursework timeframe
- focused on one complete playable path
- built around a limited number of puzzles and one major encounter

The goal is not to build a large horror game, but to create a polished short experience with a clear identity.

---

## 13. What Makes the Idea Interesting

The main interesting point of the project is:

**the tension between guidance and deception.**

Players are constantly given instructions about what to do next, but must decide whether those instructions are trustworthy.

The path that appears safest may be the most dangerous, and survival depends on recognizing contradictions before it is too late.

---

## 14. Version Notes

This concept reflects the current direction of the project.
